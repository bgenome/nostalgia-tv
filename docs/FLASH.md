# Flash Nostalgia TV

Download the Nostalgia TV image.

Flash that image onto a microSD card.

Insert the card into the Raspberry Pi.

Connect an HDMI cable from the Raspberry Pi to the television.

Connect a network cable if the television should play your Plex server.

Turn on the television and the Raspberry Pi.

On the screen, choose your Plex server or a USB drive that holds your video files.

Use the channel buttons on the television remote to change the station.

Some televisions do not send remote buttons over the HDMI cable. The station still plays.
